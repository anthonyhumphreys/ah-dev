# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Technical leaders, collaborators, hiring and contract decision-makers, and engineering peers who want to understand Anthony Humphreys' judgement, range, and working style before starting a conversation.

They are evaluating whether Anthony can handle ambiguous software work across applied AI, mobile platforms, cloud architecture, research software, service design, and delivery. They need evidence of practical thinking, not a glossy funnel pretending to be a person.

## Product Purpose

This is Anthony Humphreys' personal portfolio and showcase.

The site presents a credible public profile for a senior developer and founder: what he builds, how he thinks, where he has useful experience, and why his judgement is valuable on messy real-world software work. Success means visitors leave with a clear sense of Anthony's technical range, delivery maturity, writing voice, and the kinds of conversations worth starting.

The site should promote Anthony as a person with judgement and craft, not as a productized consultancy offer.

## Brand Personality

Sharp, practical, evidence-bound.

The voice should feel senior, direct, technically literate, and lightly witty. It can show dry humour where it helps the writing feel human, but the substance comes first. The tone should be credible enough for technical leadership and relaxed enough to avoid LinkedIn vapour.

## Anti-references

Avoid consultancy-funnel positioning, generic LinkedIn cloud buzzword soup, exaggerated outcomes, invented impact metrics, and language that makes Anthony feel like a product being hocked.

Avoid template portfolio tropes where every section is a polished but interchangeable claim. The work should be grounded in real projects, specific choices, and visible judgement.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with evidence: show real work, specific domains, and concrete trade-offs before abstract claims.
2. Keep the person visible: this is a personal portfolio, not a faceless studio site or SaaS landing page.
3. Make judgement legible: explain how Anthony thinks through ambiguity, ownership, risk, maintenance, and delivery.
4. Stay sharp without posturing: use wit sparingly, and never let style outrun credibility.
5. Prefer useful clarity over theatre: ambitious design is welcome, but it should make the profile more memorable and easier to understand.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA as the baseline. Maintain strong keyboard access, visible focus states, readable contrast in light and dark themes, clear structure for assistive technology, and forms with practical validation messages.

Respect reduced-motion preferences and avoid relying on colour alone to convey meaning. The site should feel polished without making users fight the interface, because that is rude and also bad engineering.
